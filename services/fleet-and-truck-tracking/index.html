Fleet Tracking & Logistics

Fleet owners lose money to unauthorised trips, idling, detours and theft. Fleet tracking shows where every vehicle is, how it is being driven and where it has been. We can plan the right setup for a handful of vans or a nationwide haulage fleet, and install across all 36 states and the FCT.

  • One dashboard for your whole fleet
  • Route monitoring and trip history
  • Fuel-theft awareness through unusual stops and movements
  • Driver behaviour insights such as speeding and idling
