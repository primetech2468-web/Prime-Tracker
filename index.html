Frequently asked questions

How long does GPS tracker installation take?

A typical installation takes 1–2 hours depending on the vehicle and install location. The tracker is professionally hidden and securely fitted.

Which vehicles can you track?

Cars of all brands, trucks, motorcycles and Keke tricycles, plus whole fleets from a single dashboard.

Do you install in my state?

Yes. We cover all 36 states and the FCT, and our technicians can come to your home, office or any convenient location.

How do I book a tracker installation?

Message us on WhatsApp or send an email with your vehicle type and location. We reply with a quote and schedule your installation.

How accurate is the tracking?

Accuracy is typically within 5–10 metres under normal conditions, with updates every few seconds while the vehicle moves.

Is the tracker hidden?

Yes. Trackers are fitted in discreet locations inside the vehicle so thieves cannot easily see or find them.

What happens if my vehicle is stolen?

Open the app to see its live location, trigger remote engine cut-off where fitted, and contact us for help sharing exact coordinates with law enforcement.

Is there a monthly fee?

Yes, a small subscription covers SIM data, server maintenance and 24/7 support. Message us for the current package.

How do I pay?

Payment is by bank transfer. We share the account details with you directly on WhatsApp or email when you book.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. All trackers carry a warranty, with the period depending on the device model, and technical support throughout your subscription.
