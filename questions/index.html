Basics

What is a GPS car tracker?

A GPS car tracker is a small device fitted to a vehicle. It uses satellites to find its position and sends that location over the mobile network to your phone so you can see the vehicle on a map. Read more

How does a GPS tracker work?

The tracker receives signals from GPS satellites to work out its position, then sends that position through a SIM using GPRS or mobile data to a server. You see it in the app within seconds. Read more

Is a GPS tracker worth it in Nigeria?

For most vehicle owners, yes. A tracker costs far less than the loss of a car, and it also helps with fleet control, driver accountability and recovery support if something goes wrong. Read more

What is GPRS in a car tracker?

GPRS is the mobile data technology the tracker uses to send its location to the server. It needs a SIM card and network coverage, which is covered by your subscription. Read more

Does a car tracker need a SIM card?

Yes. The tracker uses a SIM to send location data over the mobile network. SIM data, server maintenance and support are covered by the small subscription. Read more

How accurate is a GPS tracker?

Under normal conditions accuracy is typically within 5–10 metres, with updates every few seconds while the vehicle is moving. Tall buildings, tunnels and bad weather can reduce accuracy briefly. Read more

Can a GPS tracker work without internet?

The tracker needs mobile network coverage to send its position. Where there is no signal, many devices keep location data and send it once coverage returns, depending on the model. Read more

Does a GPS tracker work in tunnels or underground parking?

GPS signals are weak underground, so the position may pause. Tracking resumes automatically once the vehicle is back in open sky and the tracker reconnects. Read more

Do car trackers work on old cars?

Yes. Trackers are wired to the vehicle’s power, so age is not a barrier. Our technician inspects the vehicle and picks a safe fitting location. Read more

Do GPS trackers work on hybrid and electric cars?

Generally yes. Installation is planned around the vehicle’s electrical system. Message us your model and we will confirm the best approach. Read more

How long does a car tracker last?

Quality trackers are built to run for years inside a vehicle. Warranty length depends on the device model, and support continues throughout your subscription. Read more

What is remote engine cut-off?

Remote engine cut-off lets you immobilise the engine from your phone, where it has been fitted, so a stolen vehicle cannot be driven further. Read more

Is it safe to cut the engine while the car is moving?

Cutting power to a vehicle at speed can be dangerous. Engine cut-off is best used when the vehicle is parked or moving slowly. In a theft, work with the police and avoid risky moves. Read more

Can I track a car on my phone?

What is a vehicle immobiliser?

An immobiliser stops an engine from starting without the right key or signal. A tracker with engine cut-off adds a remote, phone-controlled layer on top. Read more

Can GPS trackers be jammed?

Do I still need a tracker if I have an alarm?

Does a tracker reduce my insurance risk?
