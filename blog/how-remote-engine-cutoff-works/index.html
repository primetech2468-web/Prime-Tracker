How Remote Engine Cut-off Works on a Car Tracker

A plain-English explanation of remote engine immobilisation, when to use it and why it should be used carefully.

Book on WhatsAppBook by EmailCall +234 905 563 3870

What it is

Remote engine cut-off lets you stop a vehicle from starting, or stop it once it is safe to do so, by sending a command from your phone. The tracker is wired to the vehicle so it can interrupt a circuit needed to run the engine.

When to use it

Use it when you are sure the vehicle has been taken without your permission. Always think about safety first: cutting an engine on a busy road can put people at risk, so it is usually done when the vehicle is stationary or moving slowly. Share the live coordinates with law enforcement.

Do I need it?

It is optional and depends on your risk. If you park in open areas, travel long routes or run a fleet, it adds a strong layer of protection. Ask us when you book and we will fit it where suitable.
