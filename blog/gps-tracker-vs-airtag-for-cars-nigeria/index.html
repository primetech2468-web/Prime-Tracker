GPS Tracker vs AirTag for Car Theft in Nigeria

Why a purpose-built vehicle GPS tracker is a different tool from a Bluetooth item tag, and what each can and cannot do.

Book on WhatsAppBook by EmailCall +234 905 563 3870

How an item tag works

Item tags such as AirTags rely on nearby phones passing the location along. In busy areas this can work, but on quiet roads or in places with few compatible phones, updates can be slow or missing. They are designed to find lost keys and bags, not to secure a vehicle.

How a vehicle GPS tracker works

A vehicle tracker has its own GPS receiver and its own SIM connection, so it reports position every few seconds while the vehicle moves, independent of who is nearby. It can also be wired to the vehicle, which allows alerts and remote engine cut-off.

Which should you choose?

For finding a misplaced bag, an item tag is fine. For protecting a car, truck, motorcycle or Keke, a hidden hard-wired GPS tracker with live tracking and alerts is the more suitable option. We can advise you for free on the right setup.
