Do Car Trackers Have a Monthly Fee in Nigeria?

What a tracker subscription covers in Nigeria, why most GPS trackers have one, and what to ask before you buy.

Why most GPS trackers have a subscription

A GPS tracker sends its position over the mobile network, so every device needs a SIM and data plan. The tracking platform also runs on servers that must be kept online and maintained. A small subscription pays for exactly that, along with support when you need it.

What our subscription covers

With Prime Car Tracker, the subscription covers SIM data, server maintenance and 24/7 support. Message us on WhatsApp for the current package for your vehicle type.

Questions to ask before you buy any tracker

Ask what is included in the one-time installation, what the recurring fee covers, what happens if you stop paying, how long the warranty runs, and whether support is available at night and on weekends. A clear answer to each is a good sign.
