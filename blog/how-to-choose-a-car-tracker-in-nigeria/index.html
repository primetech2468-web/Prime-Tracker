How to Choose a Car Tracker Installer in Nigeria

A practical checklist for choosing a trustworthy GPS tracker installation company.

Check the installation, not just the device

A good device installed badly will fail. Ask who installs it, how long it takes and whether the technician comes to you. Our installations typically take 1–2 hours and are done at your home, office or any convenient location.

Ask about hiding and security

A tracker only protects you if a thief cannot find it. Choose an installer who hides the unit discreetly and never publishes where. Also ask how your location data is protected.

Look for support and a warranty

Theft does not happen only in office hours. Ask whether support is available 24/7 and what the warranty covers, so you know who to call and what you are protected against.
